Calculation Modes Explained
Simple Interest (Monthly Basis): Standard Indian jewellery pawnshop convention. Calculates full months plus exact proportional days for partial months: Principal × (Rate / 100) × (Months + Days / 30).
Simple Interest (Exact Daily Basis): Calculates interest on exact calendar days elapsed: Principal × Daily Rate × Days.
Fixed Monthly (Rounded Up): Any fraction of a month counts as a full month of interest.
