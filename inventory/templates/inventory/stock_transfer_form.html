How Stock Transfers Work
  1. Initiate: The source branch dispatches the piece. The status changes to In Transit.
  2. Receive: When the package arrives at the destination showroom, authorized staff verify and mark it as Received.
  3. Automated Ledger: Dual immutable stock movement records are automatically generated:
    • Branch Transfer Out on source branch.
    • Branch Transfer In on destination branch.
  4. Instant Relocation: The piece's active branch updates to the destination branch immediately upon completion.
Direct Complete Option
If the physical piece is already at the destination branch (or for immediate internal adjustments), use "Transfer & Complete Immediately" to complete both steps in one click.
