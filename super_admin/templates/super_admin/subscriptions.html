Billing & Subscription Module Architecture

The JewelDesk database schema currently operates on a direct multi-tenant foundation without an external recurring billing gateway (such as Stripe, Razorpay Subscriptions, or LemonSqueezy).

In accordance with platform safety policies, billing metrics are marked as Not Configured rather than presenting inaccurate zero values. All registered showrooms currently operate under the Core Showroom Standard Tier (Included).

Billing Integration Roadmap

To activate automated recurring subscription billing for JewelDesk in the future:

  1. Define a Subscription model linked to accounts.Tenant.
  2. Connect a verified payment webhook provider (e.g. Razorpay Subscriptions / Stripe).
  3. Implement subscription grace periods, automated suspension on payment failure, and invoice receipts.
