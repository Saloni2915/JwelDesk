Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About JewelDesk

Clear, straightforward answers about our jewellery business management platform.

Does JewelDesk support BIS HUID hallmarking?
Yes. JewelDesk natively records the 6-digit alphanumeric BIS Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) code for every gold piece in your inventory and prints it directly on customer sales invoices.
How are live bullion gold and silver rates handled?
You can configure daily showroom bullion rates for 24K, 22K, 18K, 14K gold and silver. When creating sales or evaluating inventory, the software automatically estimates retail values using your configured rates.
Can I track Old Gold exchange and buybacks?
Yes. JewelDesk features dedicated Old Gold exchange and buyback workflows, calculating melt purity values and automatically deducting the exchange amount from customer checkout invoices.
Does JewelDesk require proprietary POS hardware?
No. JewelDesk is built as a cloud-ready Progressive Web App (PWA). It runs smoothly on standard desktop web browsers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones without needing locked-in POS terminals.
Can I control what staff and cashiers can see?
Yes. JewelDesk provides 8 pre-configured roles with granular permissions, allowing you to restrict sensitive revenue metrics, profit margins, and inventory adjustments to authorized managers only.
