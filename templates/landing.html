Core Features

Built for the Specific Needs of Jewellery Retail

Every feature in JewelDesk has been engineered around precious metals, gemstones, hallmark compliance, and daily counter transactions.

Inventory Management

Manage jewellery pieces, tag numbers, HUID, gross & net weight, purity (18K, 22K, 24K, Silver), stones, and stock status in real-time.

Sales Management

Record jewellery sales rapidly at the billing counter and automatically transition relevant stock status from Available to Sold.

Customer Management

Keep customer records, contact numbers, PAN/GSTIN details, and full purchase ledgers organized alongside business transactions.

Sales Reports

Analyze business revenue using today, week, month, and custom-date financial reports with breakdowns across all payment modes.

Gold & Silver Pricing

Configure live daily metal rates for 24K, 22K, 18K gold and silver with instant recalculation of item retail estimates across counters.

Stock Movement

Track jewellery stock movement, manual adjustments, sales deductions, returns, and inventory status changes with clear audit trails.

Digital Invoices

Generate professional sales invoices with CGST/SGST breakdown, HSN classification, itemized making charges, and printable PDF formats.

WhatsApp Integration

Quickly communicate with customers after purchases by sharing invoice details and payment acknowledgements directly via WhatsApp.

Custom Orders

Manage customer custom jewellery requests, reference images, karigar workshop allocation, expected delivery dates, and advance deposits.

Team Management

Manage employees and counter roles with fine-grained permissions to keep sensitive financial metrics secure from staff accounts.

Simple Workflow

How JewelDesk Works

Streamline your showroom operations from setup to daily transactions in four intuitive steps.

01

Create Your Account

Start your JewelDesk workspace in seconds. Configure your store name, GSTIN, address, and daily gold and silver rates.

02

Add Your Jewellery

Catalog your jewellery inventory with tag numbers, BIS HUID, purity, gross weight, net weight, stones, and pricing rules.

03

Manage Sales & Customers

Record transactions at the counter, select payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card), and print or share digital invoices instantly.

04

Track Your Business

Inspect sales reports, inventory valuations, outstanding customer dues, and staff activities live from the dashboard cockpit.

Why JewelDesk

Built for Jewellery, Not Generic Grocery Stores

Standard point-of-sale software struggles with metal purity variances, HUID compliance, and daily bullion rates. JewelDesk is designed specifically for jewellery retailers.

Organized Jewellery Inventory

Eliminate confusion with structured tag numbers, category groupings, gross versus net weights, and clear stock status indicators.

Easier Sales Management

Faster checkout with multi-payment splits (Cash, UPI, Card) and automatic inventory status synchronization on every completed bill.

Better Stock Visibility

Know exactly which items are in showcase trays, which are sold, and which are under karigar making at any given moment.

Centralized Customer Records

Maintain historical transactions, outstanding payment dues, and phone numbers in one accessible customer directory.

Digital GST Invoices

Generate clean, professional sales bills with legal tax breakdowns that customers trust and tax accountants appreciate.

Accessible Across Devices

Works smoothly on showroom desktop PCs, laptops, iPads, tablets, and smartphones without expensive proprietary hardware.
